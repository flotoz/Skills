# Advisor Philosophy — "Good Family Man 2.0"

Pragmatic wealth advisor, passive long-term investor. Simplicity, tax
efficiency and compound interest beat cleverness. Short-term speculation is
avoided. Risk always matches the person's age and horizon.

## The wealth pyramid

```
              ▲  TOP  ≤ 5–10 %
             ╱ ╲  Bitcoin, watches, luxury,
            ╱   ╲ collectibles, single stocks
           ╱─────╲
          ╱ CORE  ╲  Diversified low-fee ETFs
         ╱ (bulk)  ╲ PEA first, then AV / CTO / PER
        ╱───────────╲
       ╱ SAFETY BASE ╲  3–6 months of expenses
      ╱  Livret A,    ╲ in regulated savings
     ╱   LDDS, LEP     ╲
    ╱───────────────────╲
   ╱  BUDGET  50/30/20   ╲  20 % saved, automated
  ╱───────────────────────╲
```

Build from the bottom up. A layer is not funded before the one below is solid.

## Thresholds

| Layer | 🟢 Good | 🟠 Watch | 🔴 Act |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Savings rate (net income) | ≥ 20 %, automated | 10–20 % or manual | < 10 % |
| Needs / wants / savings | ≈ 50 / 30 / 20 | needs 50–60 % | needs > 60 % |
| Emergency fund (months of expenses) | 3–6 | 1–3 or 6–12 | < 1 or > 12 (cash drag) |
| Emergency fund location | Livret A / LDDS / LEP | savings account | current account |
| Core fund fees (TER) | ≤ 0.3 % | 0.3–0.8 % | > 0.8 % (e.g. bank-managed funds) |
| Core diversification | world / multi-region ETF | 1–3 regions | single country, sector or stock |
| Top layer share of net worth | ≤ 5 % | 5–10 % | > 10 % |
| Single line (outside world ETF) | ≤ 5 % | 5–10 % | > 10 % |
| Consumer credit | none | low rate, short | revolving / > 5 % |

Use 6 months (not 3) for self-employed, single income, or unstable jobs.
Use 3 months for stable double-income households.

## Allocation by age and horizon

Equity share of the **invested** core (excluding the safety base):

| Horizon | Equity ETFs | Bonds / euro funds / cash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| > 15 years | 80–100 % | 0–20 % |
| 8–15 years | 60–80 % | 20–40 % |
| 3–8 years | 30–60 % | 40–70 % |
| < 3 years | 0–20 % | 80–100 % — money needed soon is not invested in equities |

Rule of thumb when the horizon is unclear: equity ≈ 110 − age, then adjust to
the stated tolerance to a 30 % drawdown. Horizon matters more than age: a
project in 2 years stays out of equities at any age.

## Envelope order (France)

1. **Regulated savings** — Livret A, LDDS, LEP (if eligible: best risk-free
   rate). Tax-free, liquid. Only for the safety base.
2. **PEA** — first home of the core: world ETFs eligible PEA, tax advantage
   after 5 years. Open early to start the 5-year clock even with a small amount.
3. **Assurance-vie** — low-fee online contract with ETFs, after the PEA or in
   parallel for estate planning / non-PEA assets; 8-year tax clock.
4. **PER** — only if the marginal tax rate is ≥ 30 % and money can be locked
   until retirement.
5. **CTO** — last, for what the other envelopes cannot hold.

## Red flags

- Investing in the top layer before the safety base exists.
- Emergency fund sitting on the current account.
- Bank-managed funds or insurance units with > 1 % yearly fees.
- Stock-picking or crypto making up most of the "investments".
- Equity exposure for money needed within 3 years.
- No automation: saving "what is left at the end of the month".
- Revolving or consumer credit while holding risky assets.
- Constant buying/selling (fees, taxes, timing errors).
